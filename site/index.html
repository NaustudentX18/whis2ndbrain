The small promise

Catch first.
Make sense later.

The best capture flow gets out of your way. The best memory system earns your trust. Whis2ndBrain is being designed around that balance.

01

Press. Speak. Done.

A tactile, push-to-talk recorder designed to work before Wi-Fi, a browser or an AI model enters the picture.

02

Your original stays yours.

Keep the audio as the source. A transcript can help you search it—but it is machine-made and can be wrong.

03

Uncertainty gets a seat.

Suggestions should be editable, reviewable and allowed to say “not sure”. Nothing gets sent or filed on your behalf.

Warm, not watchful

A companion.
Not a spyglass.

Pip is a little visual friend—not a wake word, not an always-listening mic, and not an agent acting on your behalf. Ambient mode is intended for calm clock and device status, not continuous recording.

“Local-first” is a direction, not a magic privacy guarantee. Storage, encryption, retention and network access still need explicit decisions and proof.

✳

Trust is a build requirement.

Before any real voice data is used, the project needs a proven data lifecycle and a tested recovery path.

  • Private-network-first; no cloud upload by default.
  • Keep originals until durable receipt and reviewed retention policy.
  • Machine output stays labelled, fallible and reviewable.
  • No public exposure, auto-deletion or automatic external actions.
A prototype is not a certified product. Do not record other people without the appropriate consent and authority for your context.
Small steps. Real proof.

Build the boring bits beautifully.
